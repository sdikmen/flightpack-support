Flight Pack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Flight Pack app collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Flight Pack is operated by Aşkın Er ("we"). If you have a question, contact us at cpt.askin.er@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your first name, last name, email address and password when you create an account. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from that service, along with a sign-in token.
  • Flight notes and airport content: the notes you add for airports, such as flight numbers and dates, runways, taxi routes, parking positions, procedures, frequencies and free text. This also includes your favourite airports, likes and dislikes.
  • Logbook records: the flights and simulator sessions you log, such as dates, routes, times, aircraft type and registration, names of crew you enter, landings and remarks.
  • Layover content and photos: the layover notes you write and any photos you choose to add from your camera or photo library. The app only accesses the camera or library when you use these features.
  • Technical data: our servers may record standard request information, such as your IP address and the time of a request, to operate and protect the service.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in, including sending security codes by email.
  • To store your notes and logbook records and keep them available on your devices.
  • To show the content you share to other Flight Pack users.
  • To operate, maintain and protect the service.

We do not sell your personal information. Flight Pack does not show ads, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Content shared with other users

Flight notes, layover notes and photos you submit may be visible to other Flight Pack users. Do not include sensitive personal information in them. Your logbook records are for your own account.

Service providers

We share information only as needed to run the app:

  • Amazon Web Services hosts our servers, in the EU (Frankfurt) region.
  • Apple and Google process sign-in when you choose those options. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Shorebird delivers app updates. The app contacts this service to check for updates.
  • Maps: if you open an airport in Apple Maps or Google Maps, its location is passed to that app or website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your sign-in session, cached data and records waiting to sync on your device so it can work offline. Signing out ends your session. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. To delete your account and associated personal data, email us at cpt.askin.er@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will complete the request within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep certain information longer. Content you shared with other users may remain in an anonymised form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us at the address above.

Security

We take steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Flight Pack is intended for aviation professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
